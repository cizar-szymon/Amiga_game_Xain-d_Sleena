XAIN'D SLEENA (PC) - releases (fixes and additions)
===================================================

The release number is shown in the window title and on the game's start
screen (RELEASE x.y.z, where the credit count was; with -arcade the screen
is as on the arcade). When reporting a bug, give the release number - it
tells whether the bug has already been fixed in a newer one.

RELEASE 0.9.6  (15.10.2026)
---------------------------

Fixes:
- Jumping sideways with up (keyboard):
  the game takes the jump direction only at take-off, so left/right
  pressed a moment after up gave a straight jump. The jump now waits up
  to 3 frames (about 1/20 s) for a side direction. The direction cannot

RELEASE 0.9  (12.10.2026) - first release
-----------------------------------------

Game:
- Emulation of the Xain'd Sleena arcade board (3x 6809, 68705 MCU,
  2x YM2203) on the PC (Windows), keyboard only.
- Default start: coins inserted automatically, cursor left / right =
  1 / 2 player game, after the last life PRESS FIRE TO CONTINUE
  (shoot = go on). -arcade: as the arcade.
- -easy: on foot, hits take half as much from the DAMAGE GAGE bar (not
  in the original).
- -lives 3|4|6|inf.
- -loadstate FILE: loads a state of the arcade board saved on the Amiga
  with F10 (analysis of bug reports).

Fixes (against the test builds):
- The game froze now and then (picture stands still, music plays,
  nothing works): a race of the arcade's two CPUs for a shared
  synchronisation byte.
- In the flying stages there were no enemies after a death and the
  stage ended by itself: the PRESS FIRE TO CONTINUE text change altered
  the ROM checksum, and with a wrong sum the game switches on its own
  protection. The sum is kept now.
